Model. Each venue is a small queueing system: arrivals follow this place's daily peaks and the chosen day or occasion, people stay for a typical visit time (longer when crowded, because exits slow down), and the gate limits entry. The forecast runs 40 noisy futures with sudden rushes, so it gives a chance of reaching 4/m² and a bad-case band. The controller acts on density now, in 20 minutes, and on that chance. A 0-100 risk score shows what drives each venue's risk. Real counts sent to /api/telemetry make the model correct its own arrival estimate.

Assumed. Locations are approximate; areas, gate widths, popularity, visit times and the 2 / 3 / 4 people per m² thresholds are placeholders. Replace them with surveyed values and police guidance. Send real counts to POST /api/telemetry.

This is a decision-support prototype, not a substitute for trained crowd managers and the local authority.
